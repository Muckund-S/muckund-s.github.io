## Overview

I developed a full design brief for a rental property in Kawartha Lakes, built around energy-efficient appliances, LED lighting, and radiant in-floor heating.

## Drawings

I produced detailed AutoCAD architectural elevations (front, back, and side) at 3/16″ = 1′-0″ scale, along with supporting project documentation.

## Scoping

Market research and financial analysis were used to scope the project against a $250k–$350k land-cost estimate.
