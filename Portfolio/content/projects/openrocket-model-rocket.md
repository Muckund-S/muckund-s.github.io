## Overview

I engineered a small-scale model rocket for aerodynamic stability and weight efficiency, using OpenRocket to tune the airframe geometry to a 1.5–2.0 caliber stability margin.

## Design trade-off

I modeled custom 3D-printed fins and a nose cone to hit specific centre-of-mass and centre-of-pressure targets. Mass was added deliberately to improve stability, at the cost of maximum altitude.

## Launch

The rocket was launched and recovered at a predicted apogee of about 300 ft, validating the OpenRocket simulation against post-flight data.
