## Overview

I drafted precise L-shaped floor plans and elevation drawings in AutoCAD for a high-end residential kitchen renovation.

## Budget

The brief set a strict $30,000 budget. Through careful material sourcing I delivered every requirement at **$26,090.96**, which is 13% under budget.

## Prototyping and sustainability

I created laser-cut files for physical prototyping and built in sustainable design elements to maximize natural light and reduce long-term electricity costs.
