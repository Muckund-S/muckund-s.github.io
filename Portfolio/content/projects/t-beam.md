## Overview

I designed a reinforced concrete beam with an inverted T-section that reduced concrete volume by more than 20% versus a standard rectangular beam while maintaining load-bearing capacity.

## Reinforcement

The reinforcement cage uses longitudinal tensile bars and variably spaced stirrups, spaced tighter near the ends to manage peak shear stress per ACI 318-19. Concrete is concentrated in the high-stress zones, and #12 steel is reserved for the tension zones only, so the T-section's efficiency becomes targeted material placement rather than uniform overdesign.
