- Designed a purely mechanical system that converts hand-crank input into coordinated multi-axis claw motion through gears, linkages, and driving belts.
- Modelled every subsystem in CAD, with a focus on clean integration between the moving assemblies.
- Prioritized design for manufacturing and assembly so parts could be fabricated and put together quickly and repeatably.
