## Overview

I led the design and fabrication of an electricity-free, 3-axis claw machine. A handwheel-driven input system with a 2:1 gear ratio moves the claw, and positional drift stays below 5 mm across an 80 cm playfield.

## Design and drawings

The full assembly was modeled in SolidWorks, with fully constrained 2D engineering drawings carrying dimensions, tolerances, and datum references for fabrication.

## Fabrication

I machined and assembled the gantry, the three-pronged gripper, and a syringe-driven pneumatic scissor lift into one working assembly.

## Validation

The prototype was validated against ASTM F963 and EU Toy Safety Directive requirements. It achieved an **80% object retention rate**, under **5 mm** of positional drift, and all input forces below **30 N**.
