## Overview

I engineered a multi-axis mechanical arm driven by a closed-loop, syringe-actuated hydraulic system for precision material handling. It has a biomimetic three-finger claw and a custom rotary belt base that expands the workspace.

## Drawings and team build

I produced the AutoCAD technical drawings for the laser-cut plywood components and coordinated fabrication sequencing and subsystem integration across a multi-person team build.

## Results

The arm achieves a **300° range of motion** and an **11.5 in. maximum vertical reach**, a **45%** increase in workspace coverage over fixed-base designs. Workflow optimization cut cycle time by **65%** (22 s to 7.6 s), and the arm lifted and moved material over obstacles without loss of stability.
