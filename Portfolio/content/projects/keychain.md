## Overview

I modeled a four-component aluminum keychain assembly (top plate, bottom plate, spacer, and keyring connector) in SolidWorks, using fully constrained, dimensionally complete sketches with clear primary, secondary, and tertiary datums.

## GD&T and fit

GD&T fundamentals (hole position, concentricity, and thickness tolerances) were applied so that the fasteners, gear, and keyring interfaces align properly.

## Manufacture

I machined the components on manual and CNC equipment and assembled the final product, confirming an accurate, reliable fit across all interfaces.
