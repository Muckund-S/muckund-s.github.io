- Modelled stability, centre of mass vs. centre of pressure, fin geometry, and predicted flight performance in OpenRocket.
- Designed the fins and nose cone in CAD and fabricated them with 3D printing.
- Launched the rocket and compared real flight results against the simulation to close the design loop.
