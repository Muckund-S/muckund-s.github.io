- Designed an inverted T-section to cut material use by more than 20% compared with a rectangular beam.
- Specified longitudinal tensile reinforcement and variably spaced stirrups to resist shear stress at the beam ends.
- Built custom cardboard formwork to hold the geometry during curing.
